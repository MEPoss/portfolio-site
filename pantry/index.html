A notebook that does the maths for you.

“A list should get out of your way, and then remember everything for you.”

I built Pantry because every shopping app I tried wanted an account first and a subscription second. I wanted the opposite: a page of paper that opens instantly, and quietly keeps the prices I pay so next week's shop is a little cheaper.

It's a small SwiftUI app, made slowly and on purpose. Everything stays on the phone.

— Ugo Possenti

Add it in a second. Cross it out with a flourish.

Pull the page down and type. That's the whole add flow. No sheet, no form, no “save” button.

  • Pull to add. A blank notebook line appears at the top, with suggestions from items you already know.
  • Swipe for more. Delete, or reduce a quantity from ×4 to ×3 without removing the item.
  • Undo, always. Removed something by accident? A banner brings it back.
  • Satisfying ticks. The line draws across the name as you check it off, with a soft haptic.

Which store was cheapest? Pantry remembers.

Log what you paid once. From then on, every row on your list shows the best price you've ever found, and where.

  • Per-unit maths. Pay €5.99 for a litre, or €2.99 for 500 ml. Pantry compares the unit price, not the sticker.
  • Stores side by side. Every purchase, sorted cheapest first, editable in place.
  • Your stores, your names. Keep a list of your supermarkets and pick them with one tap.
  • Any currency. Euro, dollar, pound, switchable any time.

Made to be read at arm's length, in a bright aisle or a dark kitchen.

Warm paper by day, near-black by night. Try the accent colours below: this page follows along.

  • Night mode on a schedule. Or follow the system, whichever you prefer.
  • Readable by design. Text scales with your system size setting, and colours are tuned for contrast in both modes.

Nothing leaves your phone.

Pantry has no sign-up, no servers and no analytics. Your lists and prices are stored on your device and nowhere else.

  • No account or login
  • No tracking, no ads
  • No network requests
  • Works fully offline
